# KeepSync Notes logo prompt pack

Project description: KeepSync Notes imports Google Keep Takeout archives and other note formats into a private local desktop library with search, editing, backups, and portable exports.

Design language: organized note cards, clean transfer paths, and confident two-way movement. Use deep navy, electric blue, fresh green, and white. Avoid the Google Keep lightbulb, Google brand colors, cloud logos, mascots, or generic clip art.

## Minimal icon

```text
Create a minimal software icon for KeepSync Notes, a local desktop app that imports exported notes into a searchable private library. Draw one compact note card with two opposing transfer arrows. Use flat geometric shapes, generous negative space, rounded corners, and a silhouette that remains clear from 16 px to 128 px. Use deep navy, electric blue, fresh green, and white. No text, letters, numbers, product screenshot, app window, cloud logo, Google Keep lightbulb, or decorative effects.

Output must be a 512x512 true transparent PNG in RGBA format. Everything outside the mark must have alpha = 0. Do not render a checkerboard or any white, gray, black, colored, or textured background. Do not simulate transparency. Only the mark may contain visible pixels. Verify that the finished PNG is RGBA and that every corner pixel has alpha = 0.
```

## Review checklist

- Inspect the result on white, deep navy, and transparent checkerboard previews.
- Check 16 px, 32 px, 64 px, and 256 px sizes.
- Reject flattened backgrounds, muddy gradients, tiny interior details, or shapes that disappear in monochrome.
- Keep every attempt in the dated concept folder before selecting a final mark.
