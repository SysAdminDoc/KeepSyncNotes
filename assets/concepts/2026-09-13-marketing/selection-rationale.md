# Brand selection

## Selected direction

The note relay mark is the final identity. A dark note card provides the stable silhouette. Blue and green arrows show movement in both directions without borrowing the Google Keep lightbulb or another vendor mark.

It won the review for four reasons:

- The subject is obvious at a glance: a note is moving between systems.
- Its blue, green, navy, and white palette matches the product interface on light and dark surfaces.
- The mark remains recognizable at 16 px because the card, three lines, and arrows stay distinct.
- It can be drawn as clean vector geometry and converted to a real transparent RGBA PNG.

## Rejected directions

- Minimal flow was clear but too close to a generic file transfer icon.
- App stack communicated completion, not synchronization.
- The first wordmark inherited the generic minimal-flow mark and lost contrast on dark backgrounds.
- Orbit emblem felt busier and less current at small sizes.
- Abstract index looked more like filter settings than note ownership.

The original opaque icon remains under `originals`. Every vector candidate and rendered comparison is kept in this folder.
